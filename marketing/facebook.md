## Post 2 — how it works

How a 2DE bargain works:

1. You find it online.
2. You order on the website.
3. We secure it from our supplier.
4. We check it before it leaves us.
5. We ship it.
6. You get the bargain.

If we cannot secure it, the order is cancelled and any payment is refunded under our refund policy. Refunds are not instant.
